# Competitive landscape: fragrance dupe-finder / clone-alternative sites and tools (UK focus, as of 1 Nov 2026)

Research method note: Facts come from each site's own homepage or vendor pages fetched on 2026-11-01, unless stated otherwise. Search-engine summaries are marked "(search snippet)" and are weaker evidence. ScentVerdict pages returned HTTP 403 and AltScent returned HTTP 503 when fetched, so those profiles rely on secondary mentions. No Similarweb or other traffic data was accessible, so traffic is a gap for every competitor.

## 1. Named competitors: profiles

### Takeaway
Of the six named competitors, only The Sniff Test is clearly UK-native, pricing in GBP. It is also the largest by data volume, with 13,207 clones mapped to 5,987 originals. FragDNA is the closest feature match to "Perfume Intelligence": it normalises prices per 100ml, has about 10.7k originals and 7.8k "twins", and monetises with ads and affiliate links, but it prices in "$". Haus of Dupes, PerfumeCloneFinder and Scentfile are smaller sites that price in USD and are US-leaning. Scent Atlas is a small discovery site seen only on Product Hunt. AltScent could not be verified.

### Cited Findings

**The Sniff Test (thesnifftest.co.uk), UK**
- Positions itself as "the definitive fragrance clone and dupe database" and claims to be the UK's broadest. It claims 5,987 designer and niche fragrances, 13,207 rated clones and dupes, and 560+ brands — [The Sniff Test homepage](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/)
- The user's "13,000+ alternatives to ~6,000 perfumes" figure is confirmed as 13,207 and 5,987 — [The Sniff Test](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/)
- Each comparison page ranks clones by closeness to the original and highlights the cheapest price per ml, not per 100ml. Community members score each clone on similarity, strength, longevity and sillage. Users can browse by note, accord, gender, brand, vendor and perfumer. Creed Aventus has the most rated alternatives, at 19 — [The Sniff Test](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/); perfumer pages, e.g. [/perfumer/ernest-beaux/](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/perfumer/ernest-beaux/)
- Its vendor coverage is heavily weighted to oil-based clone houses:

  | Vendor | Clones | Price range | Average similarity |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Universal Perfumes and Cosmetics (US) | 5,515 | £0.19–£673 | 7.4 |
  | Generic Perfumes (UK) | 3,702 | £4–£749.99 | 7.3 |
  | Perfume Parlour (UK) | 2,441 | £3–£22 | 6.7 |
  | Arabian Perfume Oil (UK) | 1,268 | £3.99–£18.29 | 7.3 |
  | The Essence Vault (UK) | 281 | £4.99–£39.99 | 7.2 |

  Prices are in GBP, with a USD/EUR selector — [The Sniff Test vendors](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/vendor/)
- Monetisation: affiliate links to Perfume Parlour, Generic Perfumes and Amazon, and Sovrn affiliate tracking on Essence Vault links. It states that "No commercial arrangement has ever influenced a rating or a vendor's placement" — [vendors page](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/vendor/)
- Says it "hold[s] no stock and sell[s] nothing ourselves" and describes itself as an independent site founded in 2024. No owner is named — [homepage](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/); [vendors page](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/vendor/)
- Weaknesses seen: there is no explanation of how the vendor similarity score is calculated — [vendors page](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/vendor/). Coverage is dominated by cheap oils, with few mainstream alcohol-based clone houses (Lattafa, Armaf, Dossier, Alt.) in the vendor list. Even its most-compared fragrance has only 19 rated alternatives, so ratings are thin per pairing — [homepage](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/)

**Scent Atlas**
- Product Hunt describes it as an independent discovery platform with about 1,667 affordable alternatives across 39 brands, and "Inspired By" pages for 126 designer fragrances. Features include community scores for longevity, projection, scent accuracy and value; filters by note, price, occasion and gender; and side-by-side comparison — [Product Hunt: Scent Atlas](https://www.producthunt.com/p/scent-atlas); (search snippet) [hunted.space](https://www.hunted.space/product/scent-atlas)
- The maker is Andrew Francis. The launch thread was about 5 months old at capture, i.e. around mid-2026. The listing shows 0 reviews and 9 followers, which indicates very low traction — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/scent-atlas); [maker page](https://www.producthunt.com/p/scent-atlas/scent-atlas)
- The founder's stated pain point: finding a dupe meant "hours wading through Reddit threads and conflicting reviews" — (search snippet) [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/scent-atlas)

**Haus of Dupes (hausofdupes.com)**
- Tagline: "The independent guide to fragrance alternatives." Claims 800+ fragrances and 3,500+ rated dupes; the browse page lists 824 fragrances. This confirms the user's figures — [Haus of Dupes](https://hausofdupes.com/)
- Scoring combines Note Overlap, Reviewer Consensus and Community similarity ratings. The homepage shows no similarity %, longevity score, or separate opening/dry-down score. Value picks show a per-ml USD price, e.g. "$0.32/mL" — [Haus of Dupes](https://hausofdupes.com/)
- Has an affiliate disclosure ("contains affiliate links… do not influence our rankings"). The Essence Vault is the only named seller. It says it doesn't sell fragrances or take brand money, has no named owner, and is active on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X. There is no UK focus, as prices are in USD — [Haus of Dupes](https://hausofdupes.com/)

**FragDNA (fragdna.com)**
- A "twin" finder that shows original vs dupe prices per 100ml with a % saving. The header claims 10,756 originals and 7,786 twins, while the database link says 10,770 dupes (internally inconsistent). Also listed: 4,623 head-to-head comparisons, 1,998 reviews, 149 dupe shops, 154 houses, 56 best-of lists and a 37-lesson course — [FragDNA](https://www.fragdna.com/)
- Features: dupe finder, "The Pulse" news feed, new-dupes list, forum, "swaps" ranked by biggest price gap, and a Google-login "shelf" that shows what the dupes of your collection would cost — [FragDNA](https://www.fragdna.com/)
- Monetisation: Google AdSense, a footer link to an affiliate disclosure, and "Work with us" options for brands and shops. Prices are shown in "$" with no currency named. No owner is named — [FragDNA](https://www.fragdna.com/)
- FragDNA is cited by third parties as a dupe source, e.g. for Maison Alhambra Eternal Touch as a Tobacco Vanille dupe — [Scento blog](https://www.scento.com/blog/best-tom-ford-tobacco-vanille-dupes-smoky-alternatives)

**PerfumeCloneFinder (perfumeclonefinder.com)**
- An "AI-powered" clone database. The homepage lists 189 designer originals, 348 verified clones and 38 clone houses. The "348" matches the user's figure, but no CSV download was visible on the homepage — [PerfumeCloneFinder](https://www.perfumeclonefinder.com/)
- The AI-generated score weights Similarity 40%, Longevity 30% and Value 30%. The site says scores come from AI, "not manual reviews." Top clones all score 9.0/10, suggesting compressed and inflated scores — [PerfumeCloneFinder](https://www.perfumeclonefinder.com/)
- Monetisation: Amazon Associates. It claims US/UK/EU and other "regional buy links", but every link seen went to amazon.com. Prices are mostly in USD — [PerfumeCloneFinder](https://www.perfumeclonefinder.com/)

**AltScent (claimed clone spreadsheet of 1,323 pairings with opening/dry-down scores)**
- Could not be verified: altscent.com returned HTTP 503 on 2026-11-01. The only third-party mention found says AltScent listed Just Jack Tobacco Leaf from about $27 (October 2026) — [Scento blog](https://www.scento.com/blog/best-tom-ford-tobacco-vanille-dupes-smoky-alternatives)
- Not to be confused with ALT. Fragrances, a US clone brand — [altfragrances.com](https://altfragrances.com/blogs/news/klone-scents-the-best-affordable-alternative-to-expensive-designer-fragrances)

### Inferences
- The user's figures for The Sniff Test, Haus of Dupes and PerfumeCloneFinder are confirmed. The AltScent figures (1,323 pairings, opening/dry-down scores) remain unverified.
- Separate opening and dry-down scoring, which the user attributes to AltScent, was not found on any site that could be verified. If AltScent's site is down, this is an open feature gap.
- The Sniff Test is the direct UK incumbent. Its gaps are an opaque similarity method, an oil-heavy catalogue, thin ratings per pairing, and per-ml rather than per-100ml pricing.

### Gaps
- No traffic data (Similarweb blocked or not attempted). There is no reliable popularity signal beyond Product Hunt follower counts.
- Owners are unnamed for The Sniff Test, Haus of Dupes, FragDNA, PerfumeCloneFinder and Scentfile.
- The live URL for Scent Atlas was not found.
- The PerfumeCloneFinder CSV and the AltScent spreadsheet were not located.

## 2. Other notable players

### Takeaway
The adjacent landscape includes algorithmic dupe indexes (ScentVerdict, Skinsort, Scentfile, Dupe.com), community databases (Parfumo and Fragrantica similarity votes), AI apps (Fraganty, ScentMatch, Sillage, WhatScent), and brand-run catalogues (Perfume Parlour, Oil Perfumery). Most of them are US-priced, rely on AI or algorithms, or are commercially interested.

### Cited Findings

**ScentVerdict (scentverdict.com), UK-oriented**
- Runs a "UK Perfume Dupe Index" with a live match number per pair. Its "Dupe League" ranks clone houses, e.g. Perfume Parlour with 2,468 dupes across 2,310 originals, and Oil Perfumery with 297 originals cloned — (search snippet) [ScentVerdict Dupe League](https://scentverdict.com/dupes/league)
- Shows prices per 100ml so different bottle sizes compare like-for-like — (search snippet) [ScentVerdict Dupe Index](https://scentverdict.com/dupes?p=221)
- Published "UK Perfume Dupes Compared: Algorithm vs Real Reviews" (2026). A snippet says it scored 100 UK dupes and the algorithm agreed with real reviews 86 of 100 times — (search snippet) [ScentVerdict article](https://scentverdict.com/articles/perfume-dupes-uk-algorithm-vs-real-reviews-2026)
- Publishes "Best Dior Sauvage Dupes UK 2026" articles. Its scores are algorithmic, based on fragrance profile — (search snippets) [ScentVerdict articles](https://scentverdict.com/articles?tag=dior); [ScentVerdict dupes page](https://scentverdict.com/perfume/prada-prada-paradoxe-radical-essence-parfum/dupes)
- Has a Product Hunt listing — [hunted.space](https://webmail.hunted.space/product/scentverdict)
- HTTP 403 blocked direct fetching, so monetisation and ownership were not verified.

**Scentfile (scentfile.com/clone-list)**
- A clone "spreadsheet" of 404 originals, each with one top clone: 276 owner-scored, 73 provisional and 55 not yet scored. Accuracy is labelled, e.g. "Spot-on · 9/10", and longevity is scored out of 10. The site states that "accuracy and longevity are scored separately and never averaged." It shows savings in $ and % and was updated October 2026 — [Scentfile](https://scentfile.com/clone-list)
- Uses Amazon affiliate tags ("scentfile-20") with no explicit disclosure on the page. Prices are in USD and no owner is named — [Scentfile](https://scentfile.com/clone-list)

**Dupe.com, US**
- Carrot Cart Inc. (New York) runs this AI visual search engine across categories. Its perfume report reviewed 26 dupes across 306 sources using AI agents plus human review, published 27 May 2026 and updated 26 September 2026 — [Dupe.com report](https://dupe.com/reports/the-best-perfume-dupes)
- Monetisation is affiliate commission. Retailers are US (Amazon, Target, Walmart, CVS) and there is no UK coverage. Its FAQ admits that earlier numeric scores had no source and were withdrawn — [Dupe.com report](https://dupe.com/reports/the-best-perfume-dupes)

**Skinsort, US**
- Runs a dupe finder for any product, with percentage matches split into overall, attribute and ingredient match. For example, Glossier You is a 96% overall match to Fine'ry Madame body mist. The search summary says its data was last updated in 2024–25 — (search snippets) [Skinsort Fine'ry dupes](https://skinsort.com/products/fine-ry/dupes); [Skinsort Fine'ry Madame](https://skinsort.com/products/fine-ry/madame-women-s-body-mist/dupes)

**Dupe Cabinet**
- Indexes about 3,000 clone products — (search snippet) [Dupe Cabinet](https://dupecabinet.com/)

**Parfumo and Fragrantica (community databases)**
- Parfumo has a "Similar" button where users add and vote on similar fragrances. Forum members complain that some listed "similar" entries smell nothing alike — (search snippets) [Parfumo forum](https://www.parfumo.com/forums/post/109905); [Parfumo forum](https://www.parfumo.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=109878)
- Fragrantica's "this perfume reminds me of" votes are used by Redditors as a first check — (search snippet) [r/Perfumes dupe megathread mirror](https://nyc1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/Perfumes/comments/1ilygml/dupe_request_megathread_february_10_2025)
- Fragrantica also hosts clone discussion threads — [Fragrantica clone thread](https://beta.fragrantica.com/board/viewtopic.php?id=343975&p=2)
- The r/fragranceclones subreddit maintains a user-maintained clone list — (search snippet) [ScentVerdict directory: r/fragranceclones](https://scentverdict.com/directory/community/r-fragranceclones)

**AI and recommender apps**
- Fraganty: an AI advisor with 109K+ perfumes, affordable dupes and olfactory pyramids — (search snippet) [Product Hunt: Fraganty](https://www.producthunt.com/p/fraganty)
- ScentMatch: has a dupe-finder page — [ScentMatch site](https://harelos.github.io/scentmatchwebsite/pages/dupe-finder.html)
- Sillage: powered by "Nez AI", it adapts suggestions to the weather and to your sillage and longevity ratings — (search snippet) [Cosmetics Business](https://www.cosmeticsbusiness.com/what-are-the-top-ai-fragrance-recommendation-platforms)
- Other apps include "Mon parfum idéal" (dupes plus price comparison), Perfume Picks, PerfumOS and PerScent Pro — [mwm.ai Mon parfum idéal](https://mwm.ai/apps/mon-parfum-ideal/6739297957); [mwm.ai Perfume Picks](https://mwm.ai/apps/perfume-picks/6774184221); [PerScent Pro](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/perscent-pro/id6745906018)
- Flagrance is listed as inactive — [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/startups/flagrance.md)
- WhatScent: a free iOS and Android app with similar-perfume pages, a Fragrance Finder and a compare tool. Its own magazine promotes it and gives no data size — [WhatScent magazine](https://whatscent.app/magazine/best-app-to-find-similar-perfumes-2026?hl=en)
- Dupe Fragrance (Product Hunt) has 0 upvotes and 3 followers — [hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/dupe-fragrance)

**Brand-run catalogues (conflicted)**
- Perfume Parlour (Leicester, est. 2004) maps its own products to the designer originals they replicate — (search snippet) [ScentVerdict: Perfume Parlour](https://scentverdict.com/brand/perfume-parlour)
- Oil Perfumery sells alcohol-free perfume oils — [Thingtesting](https://thingtesting.com/brands/oil-perfumery); [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/oilperfumery.com?page=10)
- ALT. Fragrances (US) sells "Klone Scents" and inspired-by perfumes — [altfragrances.com](https://altfragrances.com/collections/klone-scents?page=5)
- No Dossier or Fine'ry dupe-finder tool was found. A third-party "fine'ry perfume dupes" chatbot on Shapes is not official — [Shapes](https://shapes.inc/fineryperfum-ai)

**Mainstream press**
- A Which? (UK) blind test found a dupe preferred to the original and 70% cheaper. This shows UK consumer-body interest in blind-test evidence — [Which? press release](https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/which-perfume-tests-reveal-the-dupe-that-s-better-than-the-original-and-70-cheaper-aorxK9Z3sPVv) (title seen in search results only; article not fetched)

### Inferences
- In the UK, ScentVerdict and The Sniff Test are the most direct rivals: both are UK-oriented, both price per ml or per 100ml, and both rank clone houses.
- Parfumo and Fragrantica similarity votes are popular but noisy, with no prices and no dupe-specific quality data.

### Gaps
- Basenotes dupe features were not researched.
- Scentbird, Scent Box and UK decant subscriptions were not researched in this pass.
- Perfumist was not found.
- A Dossier dupe finder was not confirmed.
- Traffic numbers were not found for any of the sites above.

## 3. Ownership: clone-brand-owned vs independent, and market gaps

### Takeaway
All the leading dupe-finder sites claim independence, but nearly all earn affiliate commission from clone sellers or Amazon, and none publish an owner name. True conflict of interest, where the finder sells its own clones, applies to brand catalogues such as Perfume Parlour, Oil Perfumery and ALT. Gaps across the market include:
- verified, live UK prices from mainstream retailers
- per-100ml normalisation in GBP
- batch tracking
- blind-test data
- separate opening and dry-down scores
- sample and decant availability
- a transparent similarity method

### Cited Findings
- **The Sniff Test:** independent with no stock, but takes affiliate commission from Perfume Parlour, Generic Perfumes, Amazon and Essence Vault (Sovrn), the vendors it rates — [The Sniff Test vendors](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/vendor/)
- **Haus of Dupes:** no sales and no brand money, but uses affiliate links and Essence Vault is the only named seller — [Haus of Dupes](https://hausofdupes.com/)
- **FragDNA:** AdSense plus an affiliate disclosure and paid "work with us" options for brands and shops — [FragDNA](https://www.fragdna.com/)
- **PerfumeCloneFinder and Scentfile:** Amazon Associates. Scentfile shows no visible disclosure — [PerfumeCloneFinder](https://www.perfumeclonefinder.com/); [Scentfile](https://scentfile.com/clone-list)
- **Dupe.com:** affiliate links, with withdrawn unsourced scores — [Dupe.com](https://dupe.com/reports/the-best-perfume-dupes)
- **Brand-owned catalogues:** Perfume Parlour, Oil Perfumery and ALT. map their own products to originals — [ScentVerdict: Perfume Parlour](https://scentverdict.com/brand/perfume-parlour); [ALT.](https://altfragrances.com/blogs/news/klone-scents-the-best-affordable-alternative-to-expensive-designer-fragrances)
- **Evidence of gaps:**
  - Most sites price in USD or "$": Haus of Dupes, FragDNA, PerfumeCloneFinder, Scentfile, Dupe.com — sources above.
  - The Sniff Test highlights per-ml, not per-100ml, prices — [The Sniff Test](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/)
  - No similarity-score methodology is shown on The Sniff Test's vendor page — [vendors](https://thesnifftest.co.uk/vendor/)
  - PerfumeCloneFinder's scores are AI-generated and not manual — [PerfumeCloneFinder](https://www.perfumeclonefinder.com/)
  - Scentfile has 55 top matches unscored and 73 provisional — [Scentfile](https://scentfile.com/clone-list)
  - Parfumo users dispute "similar" listings — [Parfumo forum](https://www.parfumo.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=109878)
  - Which? showed demand for blind-test evidence — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/which-perfume-tests-reveal-the-dupe-that-s-better-than-the-original-and-70-cheaper-aorxK9Z3sPVv)

### Inferences
- A defensible position for Perfume Intelligence would combine several things. It would show live GBP prices normalised per 100ml across UK retailers in the £0–£30 and £40–£100 bands. It would publish a scoring methodology with separate opening, dry-down and longevity scores. It would add blind-test panels, batch and reformulation notes, and sample or decant availability. It would disclose affiliate relationships fully, with rankings that are visibly independent of commission.
- Coverage of mainstream alcohol-based clone houses sold in UK shops appears under-served relative to cheap oil houses, judging by The Sniff Test's vendor list. Examples include Lattafa, Armaf, Maison Alhambra, Dossier, and supermarket and high-street dupes such as Aldi, Lidl, Boots, Next and Zara.
- Batch tracking was not seen on any competitor checked. This is an absence of evidence rather than a confirmed gap.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence was found of any competitor offering batch tracking, sample/decant links, or opening vs dry-down scores. AltScent reportedly has the latter but could not be checked.
- Monetisation and ownership details for ScentVerdict and Scent Atlas were not verified.
