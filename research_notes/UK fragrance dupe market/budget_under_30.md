# Budget fragrance alternatives in the UK (£0–£30): brands, dupe pairings, prices and reputation

Research date: 1 November 2026. Unless flagged, prices are retailer or blog listings that were indexed or published in 2025–2026. Similarity claims are **opinion**, from reviewers, retailers, or AI- or algorithm-based "match %" scores. Prices and listings are **fact as listed**, but they change often. Many sources are retailer or affiliate blogs with a commercial interest, and each is flagged below.

## Which brands dominate the £0–£30 band in the UK?

### Takeaway
Two kinds of brand dominate this band.
- **Middle Eastern "inspired" houses:** Lattafa, Armaf, Afnan, Maison Alhambra (part of the Lattafa group), and Fragrance World/French Avenue. Most of their 100ml EDPs sell for about £12–£30 at UK online retailers such as Royal Scents, Superdrug, allbeauty and Emirates Oud.
- **High-street and supermarket own-labels:** Zara (about £16–£23), Aldi Lacura (£5.99), Lidl Suddenly (about £5), M&S (£10–£15) and Next (about £16–£26 per 100ml).

The Middle Eastern houses are generally reported to last longer than the supermarket lines.

### Cited Findings
**Lattafa (budget lines).** These are the most extensively mapped. A UK retailer (Royal Scents) lists 24 Lattafa-to-designer pairings, updated 29 Oct 2026. Most 100ml bottles cost £11.99–£28.99, and only King of Arabia is above £30, at £33.99. [Royal Scents (retailer)](https://royalscents.co.uk/lattafa-dupes/)
- Royal Scents grades each pairing. "Type A" means heavy note overlap and reviewers make the comparison themselves. "Type B" means the scent shares a direction with the original but diverges in the drydown. [Royal Scents](https://royalscents.co.uk/lattafa-dupes/)

### Inferences
- **Two tiers.** The £0–£10 tier is almost entirely supermarket own-label (Aldi, Lidl) plus M&S Discover. The £12–£30 tier is dominated by Lattafa, Afnan and Zara. Armaf, Maison Alhambra and French Avenue cluster at or just above £30 and drop into the band when discounted.
- **Shifting prices.** Lattafa and Fragrance World prices vary widely between UK sellers (see Khamrah below). Any catalogue should show price ranges rather than single prices.

### Gaps
- No UK-sourced 2026 prices or pairings were found for Rasasi, Al Haramain budget lines, Paris Corner, Rayhaan, Primark, Boots own-brand or Dossier. None were included rather than relying on memory.
- No independent (non-retailer) UK market-share data shows which brands sell most in this band.
- The scentverdict pages returned HTTP 403 to fetching. Their Lidl, Aldi, M&S and Next figures are from search snippets only.

## Zara dupes specifically

### Takeaway
- **Red Temptation to BR540** (£22.99/80ml) and **Vibrant Leather to Creed Aventus** are the most-cited Zara dupes in UK press.
- Cherry Smoothie (Lost Cherry), Rose Gourmand (La Vie Est Belle), Golden Decade (Libre), Gardenia (Black Opium) and Fashionably London (Delina, disputed) are the next tier.

Reviewers are split on closeness and longevity.

### Cited Findings
- **Red Temptation.** It is £22.99/80ml vs about £235/70ml for BR540. One reviewer says it is not as sweet or smooth as BR540 but "comes quite close". Another UK shopper said it doesn't last. [Grimsby Telegraph](https://www.grimsbytelegraph.co.uk/whats-on/shopping/zaras-23-red-temptation-perfume-9022401); [Who What Wear UK](https://www.whowhatwear.co.uk/zara-perfume-dupes)
- **Vibrant Leather.** It has been called "the best high-street Aventus dupe", but it "falls away in the base" because it lacks Aventus's smoky foundation. One reviewer called it thin. [Who What Wear UK](https://www.whowhatwear.co.uk/zara-perfume-dupes); [scentverdict](https://scentverdict.com/articles/best-zara-perfume-dupes-2026)
- **Longevity estimates.** AltScent estimates 3.0–7.2 hours across 30 Zara scents. Immortal Vanilla is highest at 7.2h; Red Temptation and Fashionably London are about 6.5h; Applejuice is lowest at 3.0h. These are aggregated estimates (opinion). [AltScent](https://altscent.app/best/zara-perfume-dupes-list/)

### Inferences
- Zara longevity tends to be moderate, about 4–6 hours, which is below the 8+ hours reported for some Armaf and Afnan clones. This makes Zara a "smell-alike, not perform-alike" option.

### Gaps
- Current Zara UK prices and sizes for most scents were not verified on zara.com/uk.

## Supermarket and high-street dupes (Aldi, Lidl, Primark, Next, M&S)

- **The "90% prefer Lidl" claim** traces back to a 2014 Guardian blind test, so it is dated. [scentverdict (snippet)](https://scentverdict.com/articles/best-lidl-aldi-perfume-dupes-uk-2026)
- **Aldi's own claim.** Aldi's press release positioned Lacura Luxe, Guardian and Floral Love at £5.99 each against Decadence, Angel and Flowerbomb, claiming a 93% saving. This is a marketing claim. [Aldi press centre](https://www.aldipresscentre.co.uk/product-news/get-ready-for-a-scent-sational-summer-as-lacura-welcomes-three-brand-new-premium-fragrances/)
- **Aldi tested by fans.** Refinery29 UK had fans of the designer originals test Aldi's dupes. One reviewer found a dupe changed scent as the day went on. [Refinery29 UK](https://refinery29.com/en-gb/aldi-designer-perfume-dupes)
- **M&S.** The Sunrise article mentions a sell-out that happened in summer 2023 inside a 2026 article, so the age of that content is mixed. [scentverdict M&S](https://scentverdict.com/articles/best-marks-spencer-perfume-dupes-2026)

### Inferences
- Supermarket dupes win on price per ml (about £0.06–£0.07/ml) but are generally reported to last less long. Madame Glamour is the main exception.

### Gaps
- Primark, Boots own-brand and Superdrug own-brand (beyond Skinsort's algorithmic matches) have no well-sourced dupe reviews in this research.
- No UK Lidl price-cut confirmation was found to match the German September 2026 cut.

## Typical longevity reports and quality pitfalls at this price

### Takeaway
- Longevity reports range widely. Lidl Suddenly lasts about 2–4 hours and Zara about 3–7 hours. Armaf CDN Intense is reported at 8+ hours, and some Lattafa and Afnan scents at "most of a day".
- The main pitfalls are drydown divergence from the original, batch and price inconsistency between sellers, the need to "rest" or macerate some Middle Eastern clones, and inflated "identical" marketing claims.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Middle Eastern clones appear to outperform high-street and supermarket dupes on longevity and projection at similar prices. However, the evidence is mostly retailer blogs and anecdote, with no controlled testing.

### Gaps
- No independent lab or Which?-style longevity testing was found for 2025–2026.
- No reliable UK data was found on counterfeit or grey-market risk for Lattafa and Armaf on Amazon UK or eBay.
- Reddit (r/fragrance_uk, r/FragranceClones), YouTube, The Sniff Test, The Independent and Glamour UK were not reached within the tool budget.
