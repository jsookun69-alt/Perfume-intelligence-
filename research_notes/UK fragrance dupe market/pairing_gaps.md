# Pairing Gaps: UK Alternatives for Uncovered Luxury/Niche Originals + Brand Gaps

Research date: 2026-11-01. All prices are GBP snapshots from retailer pages or search snippets **without a capture date** unless stated. Prices on Emirates Oud (emiratesoud.co.uk, a UK specialist Arabian-perfume retailer) were read live from its search pages on 2026-11-01. Its "Inspired by X" labels are **the retailer's own claim of inspiration, not independent similarity evidence**. Several of its items were marked "Coming Soon", so stock was unclear.

Method note: scentverdict.com dupe pages (which carry UK prices) returned HTTP 403 when fetched. Figures from that site come only from search-result summaries and are flagged as such. Search results were consistently thin on independent UK similarity or longevity testing. Most "similarity" content found was from commercial dupe sites (Scento, dupeluxe, Skinsort algorithmic matches).

## Q1. Alternatives per original (£0–£30 and £40–£100 bands)

### Takeaway
Emirates Oud UK alone gives a named, priced, retailer-labelled clone in the £0–£30 band for 14 of the 16 originals. Lost Cherry and Another 13 are the exceptions (Fragrance World and Zara options come from other sources), and **Herod has no clone found at all**. The £40–£100 band is much thinner. Only Armaf Club de Nuit Oud (Erba Pura, £44.99), French Avenue Fierte Luna (Naxos, £42.99), Al Haramain Amber Oud Tobacco Edition (Tobacco Vanille-adjacent, £44.99) and Dossier (about £77 via a reseller) were found with GBP prices. Independent similarity or longevity evidence exists only for a handful (After Effect vs Side Effect; Detour Noir vs Layton).

### Inferences
- In the £0–£30 band, Fragrance World / Maison Alhambra / Lattafa clones at £5–£20 per 50–100ml cover nearly every target. In practice, the UK "dupe" market for niche originals is mainly an Arabian-clone-house market sold through specialist e-tailers.
- The £40–£100 band is mostly filled by Armaf/French Avenue "premium" lines and US DTC brands (Dossier, ALT., Oakcha). The US brands have weak UK GBP price visibility.
- Inspiration labels conflict across sources (Armaf CDN Oud: Erba Pura vs Oud Wood). Labels should not be treated as similarity scores.

### Gaps
- Independent similarity and longevity tests (Fragrantica votes, Reddit) were not retrieved for most pairings. Treat Emirates Oud labels as claims only.
- PDM Herod: no UK clone found. Oud Wood and Another 13: no specialist-retailer-labelled clone found. Valaya: no full-bottle sub-£30 price found.
- No verified GBP price for any £40–£100 alternative to Bleu de Chanel, Good Girl, Le Male Elixir, Green Irish Tweed, Herod or Valaya.
- ScentVerdict pages were blocked (403), so prices from that site are snippet-only.
